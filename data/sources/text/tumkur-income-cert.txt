Apply For Income Certificate

Procedure

An application for income certificate can be made on-line or in person. Following listed are the ways.

Apply Online

Please use the Nada kacheri website through which you can apply on-line : 
Apply Online

The applicant applies online for Required certificates and make online payment of 15 Rupees.

The application transferred to operator login on respective area Nadakacheri operator.

The operator take print out of online uploaded document by applicant and forward the application to respective VIllage Acountant(VA)/ revenue inspector(RI).

VA/RI will do the physical verification of applicant and submit the report to Case worker. (VA/RI verify and give the report that Certificate can be given or not).

Case worker will update the status of application as per VA/RI report and forward the application respective Signing Authority. (Deputy Tahsildar (DT) and tahsildar(TAH) are the certificate signing authority, few services like income & residential certificates are signed by DT 7 caste related certificates signed by tahsildar)

Based on Case worker remarks, DT/TAH will digital sign the certificates and Approve/Reject the certificate.

Once certificates approved applicant can take the print from www.nadakacheri.karnatakat.gov.in by clicking on print certificates under important links.(if it is rejected applicant can print Endorsement from same above link if applicant confident about his application that he give correct document and still application is rejected the applicant go for higher officer appeal at AC (Assistance commissioner) most of time reapplying of application with proper document is best thing to get certificate)

Other ways of applying

Download and take the printout of the application form F.

Complete the application form with all required information and submit at the tehsildar office.

Then the tehsildar will make sign on it and your income certificate will provide you after 21 days.

Required Documents

Income proof / salary slip / bank statement etc.

Age proof / school leaving certificate / birth certificate

Address proof / ration card / bank passbook / passport

ID proof / voter id card / driving license / aadhar card

Passport size photos to affix in the application form

If belongs to the minority community then caste certificate

Aadhaar Card

Eligibility

A person who is employed and in a really necessity of income certificate is eligible to apply for Income Certificate.

Fees

Rs 15/- 

Validity

Valid up to a period of 12 months from date of issue.
