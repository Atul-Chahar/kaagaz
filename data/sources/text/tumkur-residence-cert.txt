Residence Certificate

Procedure

Download the application form and complete the form with required information.

Submit the application form along with required documents to the Revenue Department Officer.

After the submission of documents and application, you will receive the Domicile or Resident Certificate within 30 days of issuing date by the concerned authority.

Required Documents

Photocopy of the Leaving Certificate

Residential proof

Ration card,

Voter ID,

House tax,

Tax receipt,

Details or papers of the property which are owned by the applicant’s parents or guardian.

Birth Certificate

Aadhar card

Note: – if the applicant’s age is less than 18 years then domicile of the father is compulsory.

Office Locations and Contacts

Mini Vidhana Soudha 2nd floor, D.C. Officer Compound Tumkur- 572102

Phone No and STD Code:2278782; 2278453 and 0816

Eligibility

Only those applicants are eligible for obtaining the Domicile certificate in Karnataka who are the permanent resident or living for more than 6 years in the state.

Fees

fees for form is 45/- from setu, making affidavit is around 200

Instructions

The Domicile certificate is issued by the Revenue Department Officer under the Taluk Office, it is the concerned department of the domicile certificate in Karnataka.

Note

Domicile Certificate can be made only in one State/UT. Obtaining Domicile Certificate from more than one State/UT is an offence.

Required Information

Name of the Applicant

Father/Guardians Name

Sex (M/F)

Residential Address

Purpose for which the certificate is required

Ration Card No.

Need for the Document

Domicile certificate is very important document for all the important works. It plays an important role to every citizen’s life. The domicile certificate proves that the person is staying in a particular place from such a long time. And the individual is the permanent resident of that particular place or state . The domicile certificates comes in use for various purpose. This certificates is very useful for the educational institutions and job purpose activities. Domicile certificate is also essential for obtaining the New Ration Card,Driving Licence,Passport services etc.
