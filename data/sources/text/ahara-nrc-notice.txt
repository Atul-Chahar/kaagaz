NOTICE TO THE USERS OF FOOD DEPT. APPLICATIONS.

1.	Buy only those authentication devices which are specified by the dept.

2.	Old devices even if it is procured from the same manufacturer would not work unless it is approved by UIADI for RD services. 

3.	If you continue to use the old devices, it will not support the authentication process and you may not be able to use them in food dept. applications.

4. Each manufacturer has obtained the necessary permission from UIDAI for their RD services. All the devices needs to be first registered with respective vendors before it can be further used in food dept. application.

5.	Follow the instructions carefully for registering these devices, details for which is provided in their respective websites.

6. In addition to registering the devices for RD Services, additional APIs (application program interface) needs to be installed for its successful use. Visit their website for the essential information.

7.	Details of devices currently supported for dept. use is listed here
