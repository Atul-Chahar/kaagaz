26.
The Karnataka Shops & Commercial Establishments Act, 1961.

(State Act w.e.f. 1-3-62)

Object

This
      Act provides for the regulation of conditions of work and employment in
      shops & commercial establishments in the State of Karnataka.

Applicability

It
      applies to the areas notified by the Government.

Registration
      of Establishment

Registration
      of all establishments is compulsory. Application in 
Form-A
 to be made to
      concerned labour Inspector with necessary fee which is as under:

The
            employment of children below 14 years is prohibited under Section 24
            of the Act.

Young
            persons and woman shall not be employed during night, between 8 pm
            to 6 am.

Opening
      and Closing hours

(a)  Bangalore City 6-00
      am to 8-00 pm

      (b)  Other than Bangalore City 6-00 am to 9-00 pm

The
      name board of every establishment shall be in kannada and if any other
      language is used, it should be below the kannada version (Rule 24-A).

Penalties
      are prescribed under the Act for violation.
