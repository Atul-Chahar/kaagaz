Identification of ineligible first; issue of new ration card next

Utilize "ONE NATION ONE RATION CARD" Scheme

Ration Card System

It has come to the notice of the Department that fake news and rumors are circulating in the media that new ration cards are being issued. This is fake news!... The Food Department is disposing the already received new ration card applications only, which were received before March-2023

Aadhaar Authenticated Ration Distribution

Ration can be disributed to the card holder through any fair price shop  against Aadhaar based OTP or fingerprint authentication.

SMS Alerts

Free SMS to citizens about food graind allotted to his/her card.

Portability-Any where Ration

The card holder can draw the ration from any shop in the state.

Reward Scheme

Citizen can inform online to the department about black marketing of food grain in his/her area and earn cash award.

Public Grivance

Citizen can raise the grievance online on the food grains distribution and ration card issuals.

Transparent System

Anyone can see online the details of ration cards, allotments, food grains distribution, etc.
